Ray Marching



Overview:

This is a project showing ray marching done to make metaballs in Unity.

Role:

This was a solo project to explore meta balls through a ray marching implementation. It basically sends a ray from each pixel of the camera to see see if it intersects with a bunch of spheres made by an equation and unioned together if they are close enough.

  • Date: May 2019
  • Software: Unity (C#) / Shaderlab
