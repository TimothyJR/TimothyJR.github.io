Starlit Horizon



Overview:

An action game aimed at feeling smooth and giving depth through weapon switching.

Project:

The combat system for Starlit Horizon revolves around its weapons. Each weapon features unique combos for both fast and strong attacks. The video below demonstrates these in action, fast attacks on the left, strong attacks on the right:



The player can seamlessly alternate between fast and strong attacks within a single combo. For example: Fast Attack 1 -> Fast Attack 2 -> Strong Attack 3 -> Fast Attack 4. Additionally, the system allows mid-combo weapon switching. Players can equip up to three different weapon sets, each with distinct fast and strong attack patterns.
