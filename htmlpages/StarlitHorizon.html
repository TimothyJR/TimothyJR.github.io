Starlit Horizon



Overview:

An action game aimed at feeling smooth and giving depth through weapon switching.

Project:

The goal of thise game is to create combat system that is gives the chance for customization in how they play and avoids the player having to fight the controls. This project was developed using the gameplay ability system released by Epic Games. The player has a weak attack, a strong attack, and a set of skills linked to the weapons they have equipped. They have a set of two weapons equipped. One for their weak attack and one for their strong attack. They can weave combos between the two. This means that if you do two weak attacks, you can do a heavy attack with the third heavy attack in that weapon's heavy attack combo chain. You can have three weapon sets at a time. Easily switching between them with the press of a button. This also does not break any combos. Meaning you can really customize your combos using different attacks from up to six different weapons. Each weapon set also has its own skills.

To make combat feel smooth, the first thing implemented was input buffering. This adds a period of time after attacking, dodging, etc. that you can buffer your next action to make sure no input is missed and actions start as soon as they can. Next is to make sure that the player should move when feel they should be able to, not when an animation is waiting to end. To do this, actions can be cancelled early, giving players more control over how their character acts.
